At approximately 18:55 CST, users reported replies visible only after refresh on all clients.

Readiness reports database and Redis OK while Redis CLIENT LIST shows only one psub=2 timeline/dashboard subscriber, on worker-3; expected 14. Other broker subscriptions remain present. Ingress continues accepting timeline.itemUpsert.
The timeline listener has no application-level recovery after its iterator raises. Exact initial disconnect cause remains under investigation.

Recovery plan: preserve evidence, restart application containers one at a time, verify readiness and restored subscriptions after each. Do not change ingress, Redis data, or database schema.

## Outstanding permanent repair

TimelineBroker subscribes to all timeline/dashboard channels in every process. Redis disconnected these subscribers when output buffers exceeded the default 32 MiB hard limit. Application-level listener supervision, reconnect/re-subscribe and client recovery of missed events are missing. WebSocket keepalives and ordinary Redis health checks can remain successful after the listener fails.

Add supervised recovery with backoff, force affected sockets to recover missed events, and include subscription health in readiness. Separately assess all-session fan-out and large payloads to prevent repeated output-buffer overload. Do not consider additional container memory alone a permanent fix.

Client-visible recovery is pending user confirmation.
